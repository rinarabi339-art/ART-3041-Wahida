Baruch SSEP 2027 Mission Patch

This mission patch represents Baruch’s participation in the Student Spaceflight Experiments Program. The rocket symbolizes exploration, curiosity, and the excitement of scientific discovery. Its trail suggests forward movement and the journey into space, while the surrounding stars represent the possibilities that open up when a community works together. The circular shape brings the design together as one unified emblem, and the Baruch blue color connects the patch to the college.

AI was used as a creative tool to help explore visual ideas and develop the patch artwork. It helped translate the rocket concept into a clean illustrated direction and supported experimentation with the composition. I made the design decisions about the circular format, Baruch blue color palette, and final presentation.
